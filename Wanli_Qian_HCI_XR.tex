\section*{Research Experience}
\role{USC HaRVI Lab | Ph.D. Researcher}{2024--Present}
\begin{itemize}
\item Integrate VR/AR and language-based interaction into haptic and texture authoring, enabling users to work with physical sensations in immersive systems.
\item Develop language-conditioned generative models that synthesize tactile and vibration signals from force, acceleration, and vision-based tactile data.
\item Build bilateral teleoperation systems with tactile feedback to support remote surface perception and manipulation.
\item Curate multimodal haptic datasets spanning 200+ materials, including tapping, sliding, audio, and affective descriptors.
\item Study active haptic perception and how exploratory robot actions reduce uncertainty about object and surface properties.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{3pt}
\role{University of Chicago AxLab | Predoctoral Researcher}{2022--2024}
\begin{itemize}
\item Led SHAPE-IT development, enabling conversational authoring, on-the-fly 3D shape generation, and reconfiguration of dynamic physical displays.
\item Developed adaptive LLM-based agents that modify shape-display control scripts during interaction; investigated expressivity and usability in AI-driven tangible authoring.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{3pt}
\role{Georgia Tech Borg Lab | Lab Assistant}{2021--2022}
\begin{itemize}
\item Explored cable robots and articulated manipulators as expressive media, connecting human artistic input with robotic drawing and writing.
\item Integrated motion capture, trajectory optimization, and computational stroke representations to analyze and reproduce human artistic motion.
\end{itemize}
