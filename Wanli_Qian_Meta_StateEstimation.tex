\section{Research Profile}
CS Ph.D. researcher working at the intersection of representation learning, contact-based estimation, and robotic
manipulation. Learns latent representations of object and surface physical properties from multimodal contact signals
(vision-based tactile, force, vibration, audio) and uses them for generative modeling, uncertainty-driven active perception,
and tactile-feedback teleoperation. Validates methods through controlled experiments and human-subject studies;
first-author work received a 2026 IEEE Haptics Symposium paper award.

\section{Research Experience}
\entry{USC HaRVI Lab \,|\, Ph.D. Researcher}{2024--Present}
\vspace{-11pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Learn shared, language-aligned latent representations of contact and material properties from multimodal signals
        (force, acceleration, audio, vision-based tactile), and use them to condition autoregressive and diffusion models that
        generate tactile and vibration signals (IEEE Haptics 2026, Distinguished Technical Long Paper Award).
  \item Develop active haptic perception policies that select exploratory actions (tapping, sliding) to reduce uncertainty in
        online estimates of object and surface physical properties.
  \item Infer the contact interactions of a grasped object from vision-based tactile video and render them as haptic feedback
        to the teleoperator (first-author submission, under review).
  \item Build bilateral teleoperation systems with tactile feedback for contact-rich manipulation, remote surface perception,
        and demonstration data collection.
  \item Curate a multi-sensory contact dataset spanning 200+ materials from tapping and sliding interactions; evaluate learned
        models in controlled experiments and human-subject studies.
\end{itemize}
